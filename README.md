# Sodom and Gomorrah
## A Historical Journey Through the Biblical World, Ancient Texts, and the Meaning of a City Remembered

This website by Bradley Hobbs presents a biblical and historical study of Sodom and Gomorrah. It examines how the cities are described in Genesis, Leviticus, the prophets, the New Testament, and later historical interpretation, encouraging readers to consider passages in their literary and historical context.

## Purpose

The project follows how Sodom and Gomorrah are remembered throughout Scripture and explores historical context, interpretations, and related biblical narratives. Readers are encouraged to read the cited passages carefully and assess claims in context.
