ABOUT THE STUDY

Read the Story in Its Historical Context

The account of Sodom and Gomorrah is often approached through a single passage or a single conclusion. This study takes a broader route, following the city’s place in the biblical record and considering the different settings, audiences, and purposes of the texts that refer to it.

Rather than treating every reference as though it were written at the same time or for the same reason, the study examines each passage within its own literary and historical context.

THE CENTRAL HISTORICAL QUESTION

How did the biblical writers understand Sodom, and how did that understanding develop across time?

That question guides the journey through the texts, their original settings, and the historical record.
